# Projects (`/projects`)

**Mode:** Operate.
**Audience:** a builder with a few projects, often on a phone on site.
**Task:** pick up where they left off: see, per project and flat, what is verified, what is open and what to do next, and go straight to it. Setting up a new project stays one clear action.
**Must keep:** the demo project, the mock-mode reset, the positioning line; the product's words exactly; no eyebrow labels above headings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One square card per project and flat, each saying how much is verified, what is still open and the single next thing to do; setting up a project is the first card. It refuses a bare list of project names with no state.

OWN-WORLD: Site Paper under the drafting grid; cards as Sheet White sheets ruled in 2px Drafting Ink, square; the count in Barlow Condensed with the tally bar beneath in the three outcome patterns; next actions in plain sans; "Set up a project" as a dashed-rule card in the same size, its action in Safety Orange.

STORY: The builder sees every project at once, reads which ones have something open and what it is, and taps the one action that moves it: open the report, upload, or set up a new project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The header with the brand; "Your projects" as the page title; a grid (three columns at desktop, two on tablets, one on phones): first the dashed Set up a project card, then each project card: name, flat and stage, the verified count at large size with its tally, the open items in words ("2 open: film the kitchen, explain Bedroom 2"), and its actions (Open the report, Upload). A project with no demand letter says so and offers Upload.

FORM: Project cards with tallies, fourth on my ordered list, third of the dealt structures. seed:bc305baaffce1686. Raised by the declined hand: type on rules inside each card, a strict outcome colour system, exact tally bars, and every card's full extent visible at once. No signature motion: a working index stays still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
